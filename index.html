The Suryatapa Process

Nine Months of Sun. Four Thousand Years of Earth.

Sambhar Lake salt is not manufactured. It forms — slowly, under open sky, through the ancient practice of Suryatapa: sun-curing. The same way it has been done here for over a thousand years.

Capillary Rise

Groundwater salts rise through alkaline soil by capillary action. As water evaporates, minerals accumulate in the earth — concentrating over millennia.

1
2

Brine Channeling

Mineral-rich brine from Sambhar Lake is directed into shallow sun-pans. Subsoil brine joins it, maximising concentration under the Rajasthani sun.

The Algae Bloom

Dunaliella salina algae colonises the brine beds — tinting salt its natural pink hue and contributing iodine, beta-carotene, and B-vitamins into every crystal.

3
4

Solar Curing

Crystals sun-dry for 9–12 months under open sky, locking minerals into the lattice in biologically active, fully bioavailable form.

Hand Harvest

Salt is harvested by hand from the pans of Sambhar Sarovar — no machinery, no refining, no bleaching, no additives at any stage.

6

Clean Packaging

Cleaned and packed in eco-friendly packaging. From brine bed to kitchen with zero industrial processing at any point in the chain.
